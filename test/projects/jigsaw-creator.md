An outgrowth of another project (a peerjs/CRDT-backed jigsaw puzzle game), this is cool enough to share broadly.

Supports normal-looking share pieces, as well as much more interesting (voronoi-derived) pieces, and the ["aperiodic monotile"](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein_problem) for fun.

I have plans of supporting some escher-style tilings as well.
